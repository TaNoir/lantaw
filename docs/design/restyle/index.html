Three ways Lantaw could look

Each direction is built from your reference sites and uses the same sample Davao budget, so you're only comparing the look. They all work: drag the chart, press play, switch cutoffs. The app itself hasn't changed. Pick one, or mix parts, and I'll rebuild the real Lantaw screens to match.

A · Horizon dark

You're standing on a ridge at dawn, looking out over the year. Your balance is drawn as the nearest mountain, in front of Mt. Apo.

MercuryWindy
  • The balance fills the whole screen. Drag anywhere, or press play to watch the year go by.
  • A large, thin balance figure, plus a plain sentence saying why it changed that day.
  • A Windy-style time bar at the bottom. The cutoff floats on the right as a see-through panel.

Risk: the most memorable and the most "Lantaw" of the three, but the landscape takes effort to keep fast on cheap phones. Cutoffs and Items still need a calmer screen layout.

B · Ledger light

A calm, precise workspace. Exact pesos in a monospaced font, and a chart marked with your year's milestones.

SureProjectionLab
  • The chart is pinned with markers: 13th month, Pasko gifts, tuition, gipit day, and when each hulog ends.
  • Hover any day to see what lands on it. What-ifs show as a dashed second line.
  • A cutoffs table and "Finish lines" progress bars show the details without leaving the page.

Risk: the safest and easiest to build across every screen, but also the closest to a polished generic finance app. Lantaw's character shows up only in the details.

C · Daloy light

Each cutoff drawn as money flowing: sweldo comes in, splits into bills, hulog and padala, and what's left carries into the next one.

SequenceTarsiSure
  • Pick a cutoff from the strip at the top. Coloured dots warn when one gets tight.
  • One plain sentence, with highlighted amounts, sums up the cutoff.
  • A small whole-year chart sits underneath, so the forecast is never out of view.

Risk: the friendliest and most Filipino way to think about money (per cutoff), but it moves the timeline chart off center stage. That changes the home screen you picked in v1.
